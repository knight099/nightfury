Nightwatch / CCTV event intelligence for malls
Leave-behind · One page

Your cameras already saw it. Nobody was watching.

One appliance on your control-room network turns the cameras you already own into a system that reports incidents to your team — instead of footage you search after a complaint.

The problem

Coverage

300 cameras, 2 operators

A wall of feeds cannot be watched. Sustained attention across static video drops off within about twenty minutes — this is a known limit of the task, not a staffing failure.

Timing

You find out afterwards

A tenant complains, someone scrubs hours of recording, and a clip surfaces two hours later. By then the person has left the building.

Cost

More screens do not help

Adding monitors and guards scales the cost of watching without changing the fact that nobody can watch everything at once.

Off-site

Dashboard & WhatsApp

Description, snapshot, ten-second clip, camera, timestamp.

Continuous video never leaves your premises. No port forwarding, no inbound firewall rule, no VPN into your network — the appliance connects outbound only. If the internet drops, it keeps analysing and delivers queued events when the link returns.

What you get

Zone rules

Different rules for different parts of the mall

Draw a zone on the camera image and set what matters there. A person on the high-value line after hours, movement in a service corridor, a vehicle in a fire lane, someone lingering between cars on parking levels.

Plain English

Describe a rule instead of configuring one

"Tell me if someone is at the till more than three minutes with no staff around, and message the floor manager." It asks a clarifying question, drafts the rule, and waits for a person to approve it.

Ask

Question the record instead of scrubbing it

"Anything near the food court between 9 and 11 last night?" Answered from the event history, with the clips attached.

Digests

A written recap, morning and evening

The night summarised before your security head arrives, the day summarised before they leave. Delivered to WhatsApp and email on your mall's schedule.

For your IT and security team

Data

Video stays on premises. Only events leave — a description, one snapshot, a short clip.

Network

Outbound only. No inbound access to your LAN, no port forwarding, no VPN.

Credentials

No permanent keys on the box. Short-lived, revocable access; withdrawal cuts it off within minutes.

Identity

No facial recognition. We detect people, vehicles and behaviour in a zone — we do not identify individuals.

Isolation

Scoped to your organisation and enforced on every query, not by convention in the interface.

Audit

Everything is logged — logins, camera views, rule changes — retained and exportable.

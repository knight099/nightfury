How it works

The video stays in the building. Only the events leave.

Existing

Your NVR & cameras

Unchanged. Nightwatch reads the streams over your LAN.

──▶LAN
Nightwatch box

Watches every feed, continuously

Analyses frames on premises. Discards everything uneventful without it ever leaving the building.

──▶Events only
Off-site

Your dashboard & phones

A description, a snapshot, a ten-second clip, a timestamp, a camera. Never a live feed of your mall.

Bandwidth

A continuous 300-camera video uplink would be unaffordable and unreliable. We never build one — an event is a few hundred kilobytes.

If the internet drops

The box keeps analysing and queues events locally, then delivers them when the link returns. Detection does not depend on connectivity.

Live view

When someone does open a camera, that stream goes device-to-browser directly. It is not stored or relayed through us.

OperationsRoles09 / 12
The questions your IT team will ask

Answered before they ask them.

Data residency

Video does not leave your premises

Continuous footage is analysed on the box and discarded there. What leaves is an event: a description, one snapshot, a short clip.

Network exposure

No inbound access to your LAN

Outbound connection only. No port forwarding, no VPN tunnel into your network, no inbound firewall change.

Credentials

No permanent keys on the box

The appliance holds no standing cloud credentials. It is issued short-lived, revocable access. Pulling its authorisation cuts it off within minutes.

Identity

No facial recognition

We detect people, vehicles and behaviour in a zone. We do not build a face database or identify individuals. This is a deliberate product decision.

Isolation

Your data is scoped to you

Every record is bound to your organisation and site, enforced on each query — not by convention in the interface.

Accountability

Everything is logged

Who logged in, who viewed which camera, who changed which rule, and when — retained and exportable.
